# Skill: sorting the inbox

- A **lead** is someone who hasn't paid us yet and asks about work, prices or availability.
- A **client** is someone we already work for. Anything from a client that asks a question needs a reply.
- A **bill** asks us to pay. Note the amount and the due date in the one-line summary.
- **Noise** is newsletters, promotions, automated notifications that need nothing from us.
- When unsure between client and lead, pick client: answering too fast never lost anyone.
