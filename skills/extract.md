# Skill: spotting promises

- A promise is something the owner said they would do, or a direct request they must answer by a date.
- "Let me know if you have questions" is not a promise. "I'll send the quote by Friday" is.
- Convert relative dates ("Friday", "end of the week") to a calendar date using today's date.
- Copy the exact words that show the promise. If you can't point at the words, it isn't one.
