# Skill: writing a follow-up

- First line answers their question or says where the thing stands. No "I hope this finds you well".
- If we are late, say so plainly in one sentence and give a new, specific time. No excuses paragraph.
- Never promise anything that isn't already in the ledger.
- Use their first name. Sign with the owner's first name only.
- Under 120 words. One question at most.
